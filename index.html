Hi there! I'm

Zhong Guan.关钟

I am Zhong Guan (关钟), a master’s student at Tianjin University. I work on reinforcement learning for language models and agents, with a focus on reliable long-horizon behavior and scalable training systems.

LLM RLAgentic RLLarge-scale RL infra

My work connects RL algorithms and training infrastructure: from off-policy correction and critic learning to asynchronous rollout and distributed optimization. I contributed to Occamy-1.0 for long-horizon co-work, studied reliable asynchronous agent training in Missing Old Logits, and co-developed RL-VLA³ to scale reinforcement learning for embodied models.

JD Technology Cloud · AI Infra Lab

Leader: Junwu Xiong

Lenovo Research · AI Lab

I explored LLM-based generative recommendation and graph-aware learning, leading to work published in IEEE TKDE. I also investigated how attention mechanisms process graph-structured data, with results published at ICML 2025.
